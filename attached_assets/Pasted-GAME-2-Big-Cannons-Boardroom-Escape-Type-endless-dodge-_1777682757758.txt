GAME 2: Big Cannons: Boardroom Escape
Type: endless dodge game with Wall Street boardroom chaos

Important context:
- This game is inspired by a viral, disputed JPMorgan-style finance meme/story involving an absurd "big cannons" line.
- Do NOT use real names, real photos, JPMorgan logos, or specific real employee likenesses.
- Treat everything as fictional parody of internet rumor/meme culture.
- The tone must be silly, exaggerated, and arcade-like, not mean, explicit, or accusatory.
- Do not include racial insults, sexual assault details, drugging details, or any serious alleged abuse content.
- Keep the "big cannons" joke subliminal, cartoony, and absurd.

Character:
- Player is a tiny nervous finance bro in a suit.
- Big scared eyes, loose tie, coffee in hand, panicked running animation.
- Cute cartoon style drawn with Canvas only.

Setting:
- A neon Wall Street boardroom.
- Long conference table, glowing stock charts, HR warning signs, compliance folders, and dramatic office lighting.
- Everything should feel like a fake viral finance scandal exploded into an arcade game.

Main obstacle:
- Giant bouncing cartoon "CANNONS" with silly boing physics.
- They should be large round slapstick objects with tiny barrels, wobble animations, and exaggerated bounce.
- They should visually reference the meme phrase without becoming explicit.

Controls:
- Left/right arrow keys on desktop.
- Swipe left/right on mobile.
- Optional A/D key support.

Gameplay:
- Player moves along the bottom of the screen.
- Objects fall from the top or scroll diagonally across the boardroom.
- Avoid bad red objects:
  - "CANNONS"
  - "VIRAL LAWSUIT"
  - "HR MEETING"
  - "TABLOID HEADLINE"
  - "COMPLIANCE REVIEW"
- Collect good green/yellow objects:
  - "Coffee"
  - "Lawyer Shield"
  - "HR Report"
  - "NDA Umbrella"
  - "Receipts?"
- The longer the player survives, the faster and bouncier the cannons become.
- Every 15 seconds, trigger a "Boardroom Panic Wave" where several cannons bounce across the screen.

Hit reaction:
- If player hits a cannon:
  - show huge text: "CANNON INCIDENT ESCALATED!"
  - screen shake
  - cartoon BOING/BOOM particles
  - finance bro facepalm animation
  - papers fly everywhere
  - game over

Score:
- Score label: "Boardroom Survived"
- High score stored in localStorage using key: headlineArcade_bigCannons_highScore
- Funny game over lines chosen randomly:
  - "HR has entered the chat."
  - "The boardroom could not contain the cannons."
  - "Compliance is asking questions."
  - "This meeting should have been an email."
  - "The cannons were not priced in."

UI:
- Top bar:
  - Big Cannons: Boardroom Escape
  - Boardroom Survived: current score
  - High Score
- Instruction line:
  - "Swipe or Arrow Keys to dodge the cannon chaos."
- Game over buttons:
  - PLAY AGAIN
  - BACK TO HOME

Make it addictive, smooth, mobile-friendly, and ridiculous. The joke is the absurd viral finance meme, not a real person.
